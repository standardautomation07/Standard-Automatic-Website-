# Fire Rated Rolling Shutters — master visual definition

Governs the generated gallery views for the Fire Rated Rolling Shutters
product page. The approved hero,
`public/images/products/fire-safety-doors/fire-rated-rolling-shutters/fire-rated-rolling-shutters-supplied.webp`,
is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-18 through Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro, 2K download).
A first set built around the hero's hooded styling was rejected by Dinesh
as not reading as a fire shutter; the set was rebuilt to the heavy
construction seen in manufacturer photography of fire rated shutters, with
the supplied MS shutter reference fixing the drive. Candidates were
previewed and three approved before installation.

## The product, as depicted

- **Curtain** — flat galvanised steel double-skin interlocking slats
  (75/100 mm class per the product copy) with riveted end locks; mill
  galvanised finish.
- **Head** — the curtain winds onto an exposed steel barrel between thick
  galvanised steel end plates bolted to the wall on heavy brackets; no
  decorative hood.
- **Guides and bottom bar** — heavy galvanised angle-section guides with
  exposed anchor bolts; angle-section bottom bar.
- **Drive** — compact geared side motor on the right-hand end plate with a
  hand chain and red and green pull cords; grey wall control box with a
  red emergency stop.

## Setting

Grey concrete fire compartment wall inside an industrial building, polished
concrete floor, yellow-and-black bollards; an alarm bell and sprinkler pipe
may appear on the wall to signal the application.

## Rules

No flames, fire or smoke. No signage, text, logos, brand names, model
numbers, rating marks or certification marks anywhere — the product's fire
rating is certification dependent and is never implied visually. No people
or vehicles. Interim visualisations, replaced by installation photography
under the same filenames when it exists.
