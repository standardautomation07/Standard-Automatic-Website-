## The product, as fixed by the hero

- **Curtain** — reinforced PVC-coated fabric in a saturated royal blue,
  divided into horizontal panels by wind bars: horizontal tubular
  reinforcing bars sleeved into pockets in the curtain at regular intervals,
  each pocket reading as a rounded horizontal ridge.
- **Lifting straps** — two black vertical straps running the full height of
  the curtain, one at roughly each third of the width, over the wind bars.
  The straps lift the bottom bar and gather the curtain into horizontal
  folds above the opening as it rises.
- **Vision windows** — one row of three rectangular clear PVC windows in a
  single panel about a third of the way down.
- **Head** — a full-width square-cornered brushed stainless-steel hood over
  the folded stack. The hero carries a company mark on it; generated views
  leave it plain.
- **Side guides** — heavy black steel guide columns at each side, in which
  the wind-bar ends run; the curtain edge runs inside the guide.
- **Bottom** — a heavy bottom bar in the lowest curtain pocket with a black
  rubber safety edge.
- **Controls** — light-grey wall-mounted control box to the right of the
  door with a small display, push buttons and a red mushroom emergency stop.
- **Protection** — a yellow-and-black banded steel bollard at each side.

## Rules every view must obey

- Same blue curtain with wind-bar ridges, two black straps, three-window
  row, stainless hood, black guides, bottom bar, control box and bollards as
  the hero. A view that shows a rolled curtain, a spiral, rigid slats, a
  sectional track or stacked rigid panels is rejected.
- The folding mechanism is shown as the product copy describes it: the
  straps gather the curtain into horizontal folds under the hood, wind bars
  stacking one above the other — no invented internals.
- No text, signage, logos, brand names, model numbers or certification
  marks anywhere. Vehicles are plain and unmarked. No people.
- No speeds, dimensions, ratings or any specification implied visually.
- Interim visualisations, replaced by installation photography under the same
  filenames when it exists.
