## Rules

Same leaves, frames, hinges, operators, arms and hardware in every view. A
sliding door, swing gate, revolving door or plain hinged door is rejected.
No text, symbols, logos, brand names, model numbers or certification marks;
no specification implied visually. Interim visualisations, replaced by
installation photography under the same filenames when it exists.
