## The product

- **Curtain** — curved interlocking Galvalume-coated steel slats with the
  smooth, uniform silvery-matte aluminium-zinc finish and faint fine
  spangle; never chrome, never paint, never hot-dip galvanised spangle.
- **Guides, hood and bottom rail** — matching Galvalume finish; plain
  square hood over the barrel; dark seal on the bottom rail.
- **Controls** — light-grey wall control station with a red emergency
  stop; yellow-and-black bollards.

a dark base band and large glass panels, wide concrete apron, soft
overcast daylight. No people, no vehicles.
