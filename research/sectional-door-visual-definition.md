## Rules

Same panels, windows, hinges, tracks, springs, operator and controls in every
view. A high-speed door, rolling shutter or residential garage door is
rejected. No text, logos, brand names, model numbers, ratings or
certification marks; no people or vehicles inside; no specification implied
visually. Interim visualisations, replaced by installation photography under
the same filenames when it exists.
