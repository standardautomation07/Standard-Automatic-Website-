# Retractable Gates — master visual definition

Governs the generated gallery views for the Retractable Gates product page.
The approved hero,
`public/images/products/automatic-gates/retractable-gates/retractable-gates-supplied.webp`,
is the reference and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-18 through Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro, 2K download) with
the hero attached to every prompt as the image input. Candidates were
previewed and approved before installation.

## The product, as fixed by the hero and the product copy

- **Lattice** — brushed stainless-steel square-tube uprights with black
  caps, joined by diagonal cross tubes on pivot rivets into a continuous
  scissor lattice about 1.6 m high, which extends across the opening and
  collapses into a short stack against the boundary wall.
- **Wheels and track** — each upright on a small black wheel base with two
  small wheels and an orange guard, running on a flush ground track.
- **Drive** — in the leading section: a dark grey rectangular head unit on
  a wheeled base with a light-grey control panel and an amber beacon.
- **End post** — a fixed dark grey post at the wall where the lattice parks.

## The setting, as fixed by the hero

Modern factory forecourt: light-grey clad buildings, polished concrete,
low planting, soft morning daylight.

## Rules

Same lattice, uprights, wheel bases, track, head unit and end post in every
view. A folding-arm boom barrier, sliding, swing or telescopic gate, or a
fixed lattice fence is rejected. No text, logos, brand names, model numbers
or certification marks; no people or vehicles; no specification implied
visually. Interim visualisations, replaced by installation photography under
the same filenames when it exists.
