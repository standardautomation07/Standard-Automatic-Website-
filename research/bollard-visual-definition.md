# Bollards — master visual definition

Governs every generated gallery view for the Bollards product page, so the
views read as one product photographed from different positions. The approved
hero, `public/images/products/access-control/bollards/bollards-supplied.webp`,
is the reference and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-17 with Runway `nano-banana-pro` (Gemini 3 Pro Image), 2K.
Reference-image input was not available through the tool; consistency is
enforced by this definition and by rejecting any output that departs from it.

## The product, as fixed by the hero

- **Cylinder** — brushed stainless steel, about 220 mm diameter, standing
  about 700 mm above ground when raised.
- **Cap** — flat, black.
- **Light** — a single amber LED band directly beneath the cap.
- **Base** — a flush circular brushed-stainless ground collar set level with
  the paving, into which the cylinder retracts.
- **Arrangement** — a row of four at equal spacing across a forecourt.

## The setting, as fixed by the hero

Grey stone-paved forecourt in front of a glass office entrance, low planting,
sunny daylight with soft shadows. Commercial architectural photography.

## Rules every view must obey

- Same cylinder, cap, LED band and collar as the hero. A view that changes
  the diameter, cap shape, light position or collar is rejected.
- Rising bollards only. No fixed, removable or decorative posts.
- No invented internals. The mechanism is shown as installation detail — the
  cylinder part-raised in its collar, the sleeve below the paving — never as a
  cutaway of hydraulics or motors the business has not specified.
- No text, signage, logos, brand names or badges. Vehicles are plain and
  unmarked. No people.
- No speeds, ratings or any specification implied visually.
- Interim visualisations, replaced by installation photography under the same
  filenames when it exists.
