# Insulated Rolling Shutter — master visual definition

Governs the generated gallery views for the Insulated Double-Wall Rolling
Shutter product page. The approved hero,
`public/images/products/rolling-shutters/insulated-double-wall-rolling-shutters/insulated-double-wall-rolling-shutters-supplied.webp`,
is the reference and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-19 through Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro, 2K download) with
the hero attached to every prompt. As with the GI and aluminium shutters,
the gallery is built from engineering close-ups rather than repeated
full-shutter photographs, so the double-wall insulated construction is what
differentiates it. Five candidates were previewed (slat macro, slat
profile, guide, barrel, installation); three approved and installed.

## The product, as fixed by the hero and the product copy

- **Curtain** — double-wall galvanised steel slats, convex profile, hook
  interlock between slats, with an insulating foam core between the two
  skins; matte spangled galvanised finish, never chrome.
- **Guides** — black painted steel channel guides with a rubber edge seal
  on the curtain; rubber bottom seal on the bottom rail.
- **Head** — curtain wound on a barrel under a full-width stainless hood;
  wall control station with display, buttons and emergency stop at the
  jamb; yellow-and-black bollards.

## Setting

Chilled store / temperature-controlled warehouse: white insulated wall
panels, polished concrete floor, palletised goods, bright even lighting.

## Rules

Same slat profile, galvanised finish, black guides, seals and hood in every
view. A single-skin, perforated, grille, polycarbonate or aluminium curtain
is rejected. No text, dimensions, labels, logos, brand names, model numbers
or certification marks; no U-values, thicknesses or ratings implied
visually; no people or vehicles. Interim visualisations, replaced by
installation photography under the same filenames when it exists.
