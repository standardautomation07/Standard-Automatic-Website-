## The product

- **Curtain** — smooth flexible PVC in saturated royal blue, flat and
  taut with no pockets or seams; one full-width clear vision band at mid
  height.
- **Guides and hood** — fully enclosed brushed stainless guide columns
  with smooth crevice-free surfaces and sloped tops; full-width brushed
  stainless hood with a sloped washdown top over the roller.
- **Bottom bar** — slim aluminium bottom bar with a black seal.
- **Controls** — brushed stainless wall control station with a red
  emergency stop.

## Setting

Pharmaceutical cleanroom corridor: white flush wall panels, pale grey
epoxy floor with coved skirting, stainless process equipment beyond,
bright even shadowless lighting. No people, no vehicles.
