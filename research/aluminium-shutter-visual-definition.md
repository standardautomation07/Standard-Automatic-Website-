# Aluminium Rolling Shutter — master visual definition

Governs the generated gallery views for the Aluminium Rolling Shutter
product page. The approved hero,
`public/images/products/rolling-shutters/aluminium-rolling-shutters/aluminium-rolling-shutters-supplied.webp`,
is the reference and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-19 through Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro, 2K download) with
the hero attached to every prompt. As with the GI shutter, the gallery is
built from engineering close-ups rather than full-shutter photographs so
the material reads unmistakably as aluminium. Candidates were previewed;
four approved and installed (a slat macro was generated but not selected).

## The product, as fixed by the hero and the product copy

- **Curtain** — extruded double-wall aluminium slats, smooth and slightly
  convex, hook-and-socket interlock between slats, satin silver anodised
  finish: smooth and uniform, never spangled or chrome.
- **Guides** — satin anodised aluminium channel guides with black pile
  seals; aluminium bottom rail with rubber seal.
- **Head** — curtain wound on an octagonal steel barrel on aluminium end
  plates under a full-width anodised hood; tubular motor in the barrel (the
  copy: a light curtain that automates well on a tubular motor).
- **Controls** — wall control station with display, buttons and emergency
  stop; yellow-and-black bollards.

## Setting

Premium commercial / showroom entrance: light stone cladding, dark window
frames, polished floor, evening light.

## Rules

Same slat profile, anodised finish, guides, barrel and hood in every view. A
galvanised, painted steel, perforated or grille curtain is rejected. No
text, dimensions, labels, logos, brand names, model numbers or certification
marks; no people or vehicles; no specification implied visually. Interim
visualisations, replaced by installation photography under the same
filenames when it exists.
