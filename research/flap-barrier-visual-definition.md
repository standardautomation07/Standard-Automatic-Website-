# Flap Barriers — master visual definition

Governs the generated gallery views for the Flap Barriers product page.
The approved hero,
`public/images/products/access-control/flap-barriers/flap-barriers-supplied.webp`,
is the reference and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-19 through Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro, 2K download)
with the hero attached to every prompt. Five candidates were previewed and
all five installed (the reader-cap view was regenerated once to remove an
invented digit display), replacing the four supplier catalogue renders
that were in the gallery before.

## The product, as fixed by the hero and the product copy

- **Housings** — slim brushed stainless steel lane housings with a black
  base trim, a sloped black glass top cap carrying a contactless reader
  symbol and a blue LED line, and a green arrow indicator on the end face.
- **Wings** — clear toughened glass wings that retract into slots in the
  housings; longer wings on the wide accessible lane.
- **Sensors** — black glass panels along the inner faces with rows of
  infrared sensor windows for direction, tailgating and presence.

## Setting

Corporate lobby: polished stone floor, marble walls, full-height glazing
and planting. No people, hands or cards in any view.

## Rules

Same housings, caps, indicators, wings and sensor panels in every view. A
tripod or full-height turnstile, swing-arm gate, acrylic coloured wings or
a painted housing is rejected. No text, dimensions, displays, digits,
labels, logos, brand names, model numbers or certification marks (the
hero's own wall lettering is not reproduced). Interim visualisations,
replaced by installation photography under the same filenames when it
exists.
