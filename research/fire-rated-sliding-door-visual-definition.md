## Setting

Light-grey clad compartment wall inside a modern warehouse, steel
structure, polished concrete floor.

## Rules

No flames, fire or smoke. No signage, text, logos, brand names, model
numbers, rating marks or certification marks — the product's fire rating is
certification dependent and is never implied visually. No people or
vehicles. A glass sliding door, sliding gate or swing door is rejected.
Interim visualisations, replaced by installation photography under the same
filenames when it exists.
