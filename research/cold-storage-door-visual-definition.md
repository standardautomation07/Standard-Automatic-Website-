## The product

- **Curtain** — thick insulated flexible curtain in saturated royal blue,
  flat and taut with fine horizontal quilt seams; one full-width clear
  double-layer vision band at mid height.
- **Guides and hood** — brushed stainless guide columns with thick black
  thermal seals; full-width plain brushed stainless hood over the roller.
- **Bottom bar** — heavy aluminium bottom bar with a wide black insulating
  seal; a light line of frost along the sill and lower guides.
- **Controls** — light-grey wall control station with display and red
  emergency stop; yellow-and-black bollards.
