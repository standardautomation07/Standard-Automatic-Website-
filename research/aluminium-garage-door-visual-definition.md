## Rules

every view. An industrial steel sectional, rolling shutter or timber garage
door is rejected. No text, logos, brand names, model numbers or certification
marks; no people or vehicles; no specification implied visually. Interim
visualisations, replaced by installation photography under the same
filenames when it exists.
