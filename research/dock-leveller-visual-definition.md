# Dock Levellers — master visual definition

Governs the generated gallery views for the Dock Levellers product page.
The approved hero,
`public/images/products/loading-bay/dock-levellers/dock-levellers-supplied.webp`,
is the reference and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-19 through Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro, 2K download)
with the hero attached to every prompt. Five candidates were previewed and
all five installed (the lip view was regenerated once so the lip rests on a
trailer bed rather than on the apron).

## The product, as fixed by the hero and the product copy

- **Deck** — a hinged chequer tear-plate platform in a dark-grey epoxy
  finish, recessed into a pit at the dock edge and hinged along its rear
  edge, with stiffening ribs beneath.
- **Lip** — a hinged swing lip along the front edge of the deck that
  lowers onto the vehicle bed; hazard banding on the leading edge is the
  only marking.
- **Hydraulics** — a single lift cylinder under the deck, a small power
  pack with hoses in the pit; nothing else is depicted inside the pit.
- **Controls** — a light-grey wall control station with a raise button,
  red mushroom emergency stop and key switch, beside the bay.
- **Bay** — black rubber dock bumpers at the dock face, a sectional door
  above the opening and a black dock shelter around it.

## Setting

Distribution-centre loading bay: light-grey cladding, concrete dock face
and apron, polished warehouse floor with racking and pallets inside; a
plain unmarked white trailer where a vehicle is shown.

## Rules

Same deck, lip, finish, bumpers and control station in every view. A
mechanical (spring) leveller, a loose dock plate, an edge-of-dock leveller
or a scissor lift is rejected. No text, dimensions, labels, logos, brand
names, model numbers, capacities or certification marks; no people. Interim
visualisations, replaced by installation photography under the same
filenames when it exists.
