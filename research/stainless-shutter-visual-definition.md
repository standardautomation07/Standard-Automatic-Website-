## The product

- **Curtain** — curved interlocking stainless steel slats with a fine
  brushed satin grain; never mirror-polished, never galvanised spangle.
- **Guides and hood** — brushed stainless channel guides at each jamb and
  a plain square brushed stainless hood over the opening.
- **Controls** — light-grey wall control station with a red emergency
  stop; yellow-and-black bollards.

## Setting

Hygienic facility: white insulated wall panels on a concrete plinth,
clean paved forecourt, soft overcast daylight. No people, no vehicles.
