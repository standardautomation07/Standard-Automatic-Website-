# Rolling Grille — master visual definition

Governs the generated gallery views for the Rolling Grille product page. The
approved hero,
`public/images/products/rolling-shutters/grille-rolling-shutters/grille-rolling-shutters-supplied.webp`,
is the reference and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-18 through Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro, 2K download) with
two image inputs attached to every prompt: the hero, which fixes the grille,
and the motorised MS shutter reference photograph supplied by Standard
Automation, which fixes the drive arrangement. Candidates were previewed and
approved before installation.

## The product, as fixed by the hero

- **Curtain** — horizontal round stainless-steel rods joined by short
  vertical stainless links with rolled eyes, staggered in a brick pattern;
  the opening reads as mostly open.
- **Head box** — full-width brushed stainless-steel hood over the barrel.
- **Guides** — stainless channel guides with black inner faces.
- **Bottom rail** — stainless bottom rail with a keyed lock.
- **Drive** (from the reference) — geared motor unit on the right-hand end
  of the head box, hand chain and red and green pull cords beside the
  right-hand guide, cable to the wall control box.
- **Controls** — light-grey wall box with display, push buttons and red
  mushroom emergency stop. **Protection** — yellow-and-black bollards.

## The setting, as fixed by the hero

Modern showroom front: light stone paving, glazed shop window to the left,
white and grey cladding, planting, bright daylight.

## Rules

Same rods, links, brick stagger, head box, guides, drive and controls in
every view; a solid, perforated, polycarbonate or collapsible-gate curtain is
rejected. No text, logos, brand names, model numbers or certification marks;
no people or vehicles; no specification implied visually. Interim
visualisations, replaced by installation photography under the same
filenames when it exists.
