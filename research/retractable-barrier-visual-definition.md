# Retractable Barriers — master visual definition

Governs the generated gallery views for the Retractable Barriers product
page. The approved hero,
`public/images/products/access-control/retractable-barriers/retractable-barriers-supplied.webp`,
is the reference and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-19 through Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro, 2K download)
with the hero attached to every prompt. Five candidates were previewed and
all five installed.

## The product, as fixed by the hero

The hero shows a folding-arm (articulated) boom barrier, and the gallery
follows the hero:

- **Cabinet** — tall slim housing in brushed stainless steel with a dark
  grey centre panel carrying a vertical amber LED strip, on a stone plinth.
- **Boom** — white aluminium, two sections with red reflective bands,
  joined at a black knuckle joint; a link rod folds the outer section
  down as the boom rises so it clears low headroom, and the boom parks in
  a compact vertical stack when open.
- **Lane** — car park entry with a yellow-and-black speed hump.

## Setting

Modern office car park entrance: hedges, trees, glass buildings, paved
apron, bright daylight; an underground car park ramp for the low-headroom
view. Vehicles are plain and unmarked, number plates blank.

## Rules

Same cabinet, finish, LED strip, boom colour and knuckle in every view. A
straight single-piece boom, a lattice/scissor barrier, a swing gate or a
bollard is rejected. No text, dimensions, labels, logos, brand names, model
numbers or certification marks (the hero's own marketing text is not
reproduced); no people. Interim visualisations, replaced by installation
photography under the same filenames when it exists.
