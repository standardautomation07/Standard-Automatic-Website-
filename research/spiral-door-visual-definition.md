Governs every generated gallery view for the High Speed Spiral Door product

## The product, as fixed by the hero

- **Leaf** — rigid, insulated, double-skin aluminium panels in a silver
  anodised finish, each panel a flat horizontal slat with a slim rounded
  profile; the leaf alternates solid silver panels with clear glazed panels
  (transparent panel configuration), so light shows through every second row.
- **Panel joints** — the panels hinge to each other along slim horizontal
  joint lines; there is no fabric, no interlocking rolled-steel lath.
- **Spiral** — at the head of the door a full-width brushed stainless-steel
  hood; at its right-hand end the round end plate of the spiral guide is
  visible, with the leaf entering the spiral from below. The panels are
  carried in a spiral track so they never touch each other — no roll, no
  stacking.
- **Side guides** — silver aluminium guide columns with black seals along the
  inner faces, running from floor to hood.
- **Bottom** — the leading panel carries a black rubber safety edge and floor
  seal.
- **Controls** — light-grey wall-mounted control box to the right of the
  door with a small display, push buttons and a red mushroom emergency stop.
- **Protection** — a yellow-and-black banded steel bollard at each side.

## The setting, as fixed by the hero

External factory entrance: light-grey insulated cladding above a concrete
plinth, polished grey concrete apron with yellow line markings, low planting
to the right, bright daylight; a warehouse floor with pallets and racking
visible through the opening. Clean commercial product photography.

## Rules every view must obey

- Same silver slatted leaf with alternating glazed panels, stainless hood,
  spiral end plate, guides, bottom edge, control box and bollards as the
  hero. A view that shows a fabric curtain, a rolled coil of laths, a
  sectional door track, stacked panels or a different panel finish is
  rejected.
- The spiral is shown as the leaf running in a spiral track with clear
  spacing between turns; no invented internals beyond the track, its end
  plate, the shaft and the drive.
- No text, signage, logos, brand names, model numbers or certification
  marks anywhere. Vehicles are plain and unmarked. No people.
- No speeds, dimensions, ratings or any specification implied visually.
- Interim visualisations, replaced by installation photography under the same
  filenames when it exists.
