# Transparent Polycarbonate Rolling Shutter — master visual definition

Governs the generated gallery views for the Transparent Polycarbonate Rolling
Shutter product page. The approved hero,
`public/images/products/rolling-shutters/polycarbonate-rolling-shutters/polycarbonate-rolling-shutters-supplied.webp`,
is the reference and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-18 through Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro, 2K download) with
two image inputs attached to every prompt: the hero, which fixes the shutter,
and the motorised MS shutter reference photograph supplied by Standard
Automation, which fixes the drive arrangement. Candidates were previewed and
approved before installation.

## The product, as fixed by the hero

- **Curtain** — clear, gently curved polycarbonate slats joined by slim
  brushed-aluminium connector profiles; the lit display behind stays
  visible through the closed curtain.
- **Head box** — full-width brushed stainless-steel hood over the barrel.
- **Guides** — stainless channel guides with black inner faces.
- **Bottom rail** — brushed aluminium bottom rail with a keyed lock.
- **Drive** (from the reference) — enclosed geared motor unit on the
  right-hand end of the head box, hand chain and red and green pull cords
  beside the right-hand guide, cable to the wall control box.
- **Controls** — light-grey wall box with blank display, push buttons and
  red mushroom emergency stop; plain face, no emblem. **Protection** —
  yellow-and-black bollards.

## The setting, as fixed by the hero

Premium showroom front: light stone paving, glazed shop window to the left,
white and grey cladding, planting, daylight (dusk for the installation view).

## Rules

Same clear slats, connectors, head box, guides, drive and controls in every
view; a solid, perforated, grille or glass-door curtain is rejected. No text,
logos, brand names, model numbers or certification marks; no people or
vehicles; no specification implied visually. Interim visualisations, replaced
by installation photography under the same filenames when it exists.
