## The product, as fixed by the hero

- **Leaf** — anthracite-grey powder-coated aluminium, wide horizontal slats
  with narrow gaps, slim matching frame; slides sideways behind the
  boundary wall on the left.
- **Operator** — compact dark grey and silver sliding-gate operator bolted
  to a small concrete plinth beside the leaf; manual release cover on the
  front. The drive is shown enclosed — no pinion is depicted, after two
  renders placed a pinion at an impossible angle to the rack.
- **Rack and track** — black toothed rack along the bottom rail; grey steel
  ground track flush in the paving with grooved wheels under the rail.
- **Controls** (as the product copy supports) — photocell on the post,
  access reader on the pillar, small control enclosure with antenna.

## The setting, as fixed by the hero

Premium modern residential entrance: white rendered walls and pillars,
light stone paving, planting, warm wall lights, soft evening daylight.

## Rules

Same leaf, operator, rack, track and pillars in every view; a swing,
telescopic or manual gate is rejected. No text, logos, brand names, model
numbers or certification marks; no people or vehicles; no specification
implied visually. Interim visualisations, replaced by installation
photography under the same filenames when it exists.
