# Full Height Turnstiles — master visual definition

Governs the generated gallery views for the Full Height Turnstiles product
page. The approved hero,
`public/images/products/access-control/full-height-turnstiles/full-height-turnstiles-supplied.webp`,
is the reference and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-19 through Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro, 2K download)
with the hero attached to every prompt. Five candidates were previewed and
all five installed, replacing the three supplier catalogue renders that
were in the gallery before.

## The product, as fixed by the hero and the product copy

- **Frame** — brushed stainless steel square-section posts under a flat
  rectangular stainless canopy with a round downlight in each lane.
- **Rotor** — a floor-to-head-height three-wing rotor of horizontal
  polished stainless tubes on a central column, with a plain circular
  top bearing housing under the canopy.
- **Cage** — a curved cage of vertical stainless tubes on the outer side
  of each lane, interleaving with the rotor tubes so there is no over and
  no under.
- **Reader** — a black panel on each post with a green arrow indicator
  above a contactless reader symbol.
- **Configurations** — twin rotor (hero, perimeter view) and single rotor.

## Setting

Industrial perimeter: grey steel palisade fencing, paved approach, light
clad factory buildings, daylight; the hero's glazed entrance for the
close-ups. No people, hands or cards in any view.

## Rules

Same frame, canopy, rotor, cage and reader panel in every view. A tripod
or waist-height turnstile, a painted or galvanised-grey cage, a mesh cage
or a roofless unit is rejected. No text, dimensions, labels, logos, brand
names, model numbers or certification marks (the hero's own wall
lettering is not reproduced). Interim visualisations, replaced by
installation photography under the same filenames when it exists.
