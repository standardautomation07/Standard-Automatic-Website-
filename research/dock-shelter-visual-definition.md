## The product, as fixed by the hero and the product copy

- **Shelter** — a curtain dock shelter: a dark rectangular frame projecting
  from the wall around the bay, with a black PVC head curtain and two
  black PVC side curtains, each side curtain carrying a single vertical
  yellow guide stripe.
- **Frame** — aluminium alloy members on hinged arms so the frame deflects
  on vehicle impact and returns, rather than loading the building.
- **Seal** — passive: the curtains close against the vehicle body as it
  reverses on; nothing is powered.
- **Dock house** — the same shelter and door on a built-out housing in
  light-grey insulated panels projecting from the building line.
- **Bay** — white sectional door, dock leveller at the dock edge, black
  rubber bumpers, yellow wheel guides on the concrete apron.

## Setting

Distribution-centre loading bays: light-grey cladding, concrete dock face
and apron, bright daylight; a plain unmarked white trailer where a vehicle
is shown; polished warehouse floor inside.

## Rules

Same black curtains, yellow stripes, frame, door and bay furniture in every
view. An inflatable cushion shelter, a foam dock seal or a shelter in
another colour is rejected. No text, dimensions, labels, logos, brand
names, model numbers, wind ratings or certification marks; no people.
Interim visualisations, replaced by installation photography under the
same filenames when it exists.
