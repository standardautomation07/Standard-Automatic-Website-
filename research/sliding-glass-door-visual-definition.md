# Automatic Sliding Glass Doors — master visual definition

Governs the generated gallery views for the Automatic Sliding Glass Doors
product page. The approved hero,
`public/images/products/entrance-automation/automatic-sliding-glass-doors/automatic-sliding-glass-doors-supplied.webp`,
is the reference and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-18 through Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro, 2K download) with
the hero attached to every prompt as the image input. Candidates were
previewed; four approved and installed (the header-mechanism view was
generated but not selected).

## The product, as fixed by the hero

- **Leaves** — a bi-parting pair of clear toughened glass sliding leaves in
  slim satin-silver anodised aluminium frames, with fixed glass side lights
  in matching frames.
- **Header** — full-width satin-silver aluminium header housing the
  belt-drive operator with roller carriages on a track (as the copy).
- **Sensors** — small black activation sensor on the header face above the
  meeting stiles; presence sensors as the copy describes.
- **Threshold** — floor guide track and entrance mat.

## Setting

Modern office lobby entrance: light stone paving, planters, warm
downlights, reception visible inside; evening light for the lifestyle view.
People appear only in the lifestyle view, plain and unbranded.

## Rules

Same leaves, frames, header and sensor in every view. A manual door,
swing door, sliding gate or plain facade is rejected. No text, logos,
brand names, model numbers or certification marks; no specification implied
visually. Interim visualisations, replaced by installation photography under
the same filenames when it exists.
