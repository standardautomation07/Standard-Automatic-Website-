# Boom Barriers — master visual definition

Governs every generated gallery view for the Boom Barriers product page, so
the views read as one product photographed from different positions rather
than as unrelated generations. The approved hero,
`public/images/products/access-control/boom-barriers/boom-barriers-entrance.webp`,
is the reference image for every view and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-17 with Runway `gemini_image3_pro` (Nano Banana Pro) at
2528×1696, the hero passed as `@hero`.

## The product, as fixed by the hero

- **Pedestal cabinet** — rectangular, about 1,100 mm tall and 300 mm wide,
  graphite-grey powder-coated front and rear faces with brushed silver side
  trims, a flat top cap, standing on a small concrete plinth.
- **Status strip** — one vertical amber-lit strip, centred on the front face.
- **Boom holder** — black, on the cabinet top, carrying the arm on the
  lane side.
- **Arm** — white rectangular aluminium boom with red reflective bands; the
  hero shows a plain straight arm.
- **Reader** — a dark card reader plate on the cabinet side facing the
  approaching driver (visible in the gatehouse view).

## The setting, as fixed by the hero

Modern business-park vehicle entrance: grey block-paved lane, low clipped
hedging, a speed hump, glass office architecture behind. Overcast, neutral
daylight. Commercial architectural photography, not cinematic.

## Rules every view must obey

- Same cabinet colour, proportions, strip and holder as the hero. A view that
  changes the geometry, colour or arm profile is rejected, not kept.
- No text, signage, logos, brand names or badges anywhere. Wall slogans in
  the hero's background are not to be reproduced.
- Vehicles, where shown, are plain and unmarked — no badges, plates or livery.
- No speeds, dimensions, ratings or any other specification implied visually.
- No people in frame.
- These are interim visualisations. When installation photography exists it
  replaces them under the same filenames.
