# MS Solid Rolling Shutter — master visual definition

Governs every generated gallery view for the MS Solid Rolling Shutter
product page, so the views read as one shutter photographed from different
positions. The original supplied hero was replaced on 2026-09-19 by a
generated lead image, `ms-solid-rolling-shutters-hero.webp` (Google Flow,
Nano Banana Pro, 2K): the same dark graphite-grey shutter closed on a
light-grey clad warehouse, in the clean European industrial-door
photography style adopted for the windproof shutter hero.

Generated 2026-09-18 through Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro, 2K download)
with two image inputs attached to every prompt: the hero, and a reference
photograph of a motorised MS shutter supplied by Standard Automation that
fixes the drive arrangement. Consistency is enforced by those inputs, by
this definition, and by a preview review before installation.

## The product, as fixed by the hero

- **Curtain** — single-skin mild steel interlocking slats, a curved
  (convex) roll-formed profile about 75–80 mm high, each slat hooking into
  the next along a fine horizontal interlock line; smooth dark graphite-grey
  painted finish.
- **Guides** — dark graphite-grey steel channel guides at both jambs,
  running from floor to head box.
- **Head box** — a plain rectangular dark graphite-grey steel hood over the
  barrel, full opening width, square-cornered.
- **Drive** — a geared side motor unit on the right-hand end of the head
  box (hero); from the reference: a hand chain and red and green pull cords
  hanging beside the right-hand guide for manual operation, and the cable
  down to the wall control box.
- **Bottom rail** — an MS bottom rail as the lowest member of the curtain,
  same dark grey finish.
- **Controls** — light-grey wall-mounted control box with a small display,
  push buttons and a red mushroom emergency stop to the right of the
  opening.
- **Protection** — a yellow-and-black banded steel bollard at each side.

## The setting, as fixed by the hero

Commercial / industrial entrance: light-grey insulated wall cladding,
polished concrete apron with yellow-and-black hatched markings, a warehouse
floor with racking visible when the curtain is up, bright daylight. Clean
commercial product photography.

## Rules every view must obey

- Same dark graphite-grey curved interlocking slats, guides, head box,
  motor, bottom rail, control box and bollards as the hero. A view showing
  flat aluminium slats, perforated or polycarbonate slats, a grille, a
  fabric curtain or a sectional track is rejected.
- Mechanism shown only as the product copy states it: curtain rolling onto
  a barrel above the opening, motorised drive on the barrel end — nothing
  invented inside the hood beyond the barrel and the rolled curtain.
- No text, signage, logos, brand names, model numbers or certification
  marks anywhere. Vehicles are plain and unmarked. No people.
- No sizes, thicknesses, ratings or any specification implied visually.
- Interim visualisations, replaced by installation photography under the
  same filenames when it exists.
