# Tripod Turnstiles — master visual definition

Governs the generated gallery views for the Tripod Turnstiles product
page. The approved hero,
`public/images/products/access-control/tripod-turnstiles/tripod-turnstiles-supplied.webp`,
is the reference and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-19 through Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro, 2K download)
with the hero attached to every prompt. Five candidates were previewed and
all five installed, replacing the three supplier catalogue renders that
were in the gallery before.

## The product, as fixed by the hero and the product copy

- **Housing** — slim rectangular brushed stainless steel housing on a
  floor-fixed base plate.
- **Top cap** — sloped black cap carrying a contactless reader symbol,
  with a green LED status band around its edge (amber on release).
- **Indicator** — green arrow direction indicator on the side face.
- **Rotor** — black cast hub with three polished stainless tubular arms
  at 120 degrees; in emergency release the arms drop to hang vertically,
  clearing the lane.
- **Lane furniture** — stainless and glass guide railings.

## Setting

Corporate lobby: polished stone floor, glass balustrades, full-height
glazing and planting; a factory gate house under a canopy for the
installation view. No people, hands or cards in any view.

## Rules

Same housing, cap, indicator, hub and arms in every view. A full-height
turnstile, a flap barrier, a swing gate or a painted housing is rejected.
No text, dimensions, labels, logos, brand names, model numbers or
certification marks (the hero's own wall lettering is not reproduced).
Interim visualisations, replaced by installation photography under the
same filenames when it exists.
