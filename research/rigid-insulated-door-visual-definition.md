## What the product copy supports

Double-skin insulated rigid panels, 40–43 mm thick, aluminium alloy or
engineered metal skins with a PU / thermal insulation core; optional vision
sections; panels carried in side guides and driven vertically under
PLC, inverter and encoder control; perimeter sealing. Views show only
this: a panel-end cutaway may show the two skins and the foam core; the
drive is a geared motor on a shaft at the hood end; the guide shows the
panel end running in the black column with a seal. Nothing inside the
hood is depicted.

## Rules every view must obey

- Same blue smooth panels, aluminium joint profiles, single glazed row,
  stainless hood, black guides, bottom rail, control box and bollards as
  the hero. A view that shows fabric, wind bars, straps, a spiral, a
  sectional track or stacked panels is rejected.
- No text, signage, logos, brand names, model numbers or certification
  marks anywhere. Vehicles are plain and unmarked. No people.
- No speeds, dimensions, ratings or any specification implied visually.
- Interim visualisations, replaced by installation photography under the
  same filenames when it exists.
