# Automatic Swing Gates — master visual definition

Governs the generated gallery views for the Automatic Swing Gates product
page. The approved hero,
`public/images/products/automatic-gates/automatic-swing-gates/automatic-swing-gates-supplied.webp`,
is the reference and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-18 through Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro, 2K download) with
the hero attached to every prompt as the image input. Candidates were
previewed and approved before installation.

## The product, as fixed by the hero

- **Leaves** — a pair of anthracite-grey powder-coated aluminium leaves with
  wide horizontal slats, each hung on black hinges from an anthracite steel
  hinge post fixed to a white rendered pillar; leaves swing inward.
- **Operators** — one linear ram electromechanical operator per leaf, grey
  and silver body, rear bracket on the pillar, extending rod to a bracket on
  the leaf at about mid height; manual release cover on the body.
- **Controls** (as the product copy supports) — photocells across the
  opening, keypad/reader on the pillar, small control enclosure with antenna.

## The setting, as fixed by the hero

Premium modern residential entrance: white rendered pillars and walls,
light stone paving, planting, warm wall lights, soft evening daylight.

## Rules

Same leaves, hinges, ram operators and pillars in every view; the ram always
spans from pillar to leaf (a render with the ram running away from the
pillar was rejected). A sliding, telescopic or manual gate is rejected. No
text, logos, brand names, model numbers or certification marks; no people or
vehicles; no specification implied visually. Interim visualisations,
replaced by installation photography under the same filenames when it exists.
