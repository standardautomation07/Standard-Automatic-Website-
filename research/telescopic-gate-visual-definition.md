# Telescopic Sliding Gates — master visual definition

Governs the generated gallery views for the Telescopic Sliding Gates product
page. The approved hero,
`public/images/products/automatic-gates/telescopic-sliding-gates/telescopic-sliding-gates-supplied.webp`,
is the reference and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-18 through Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro, 2K download) with
the hero attached to every prompt as the image input. Candidates were
previewed and approved before installation.

## The product, as fixed by the hero and the product copy

- **Leaves** — three anthracite-grey powder-coated aluminium leaves with
  wide flat horizontal panels and slim frames, of stepped lengths, running
  side by side on three parallel ground tracks in a flush channel so they
  nest alongside one another when open.
- **Carriages** — black wheel carriages under each leaf, one per rail.
- **Posts** — dark grey steel posts with amber beacons at both ends.
- **Operator** — compact grey gear-motor on a plinth at the wall end driving
  a rack on the leading leaf; the drive is shown enclosed, no pinion depicted.

## The setting, as fixed by the hero

Modern commercial office entrance: white rendered walls, glass-fronted
building, polished concrete apron, planting, soft morning daylight.

## Rules

Same three leaves, tracks, carriages, posts and operator in every view. A
single-leaf sliding gate, swing, retractable or manual gate is rejected. No
text, logos, brand names, model numbers or certification marks; no people or
vehicles; no specification implied visually. Interim visualisations,
replaced by installation photography under the same filenames when it exists.
