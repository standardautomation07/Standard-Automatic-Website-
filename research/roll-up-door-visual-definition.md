# High Speed Roll-Up Door — master visual definition

Governs every generated gallery view for the High Speed Roll-Up Door product
page, so the views read as one door photographed from different positions.
The approved hero,
`public/images/products/high-speed-doors/high-speed-roll-up-door/high-speed-roll-up-door-supplied.webp`,
is the reference and is not altered.

Generated 2026-09-17 with Runway `nano-banana-pro` (Gemini 3 Pro Image), 2K.
Reference-image input was not available through the tool; consistency is
enforced by this definition and by rejecting any output that departs from it.

## The product, as fixed by the hero

- **Curtain** — flexible PVC in a saturated royal blue, flat and taut, with
  two horizontal reinforcing seams above the vision band and one below.
- **Vision band** — a single full-width clear PVC window at roughly mid
  height, about one fifth of the door height, edged top and bottom with
  narrow brushed-aluminium strips.
- **Head box** — a full-width brushed stainless-steel hood over the roller,
  square-cornered, running the full width of the opening. The hero carries a
  company mark on this hood; generated views must leave it plain.
- **Side guides** — brushed stainless vertical guide columns with black brush
  seals at the curtain edges.
- **Bottom bar** — slim aluminium bottom rail with a black seal.
- **Controls** — a light-grey wall-mounted control box to the right of the
  door with a small display, green and grey push buttons and a red mushroom
  emergency stop, cable entering from above.
- **Protection** — a yellow-and-black banded steel bollard bolted to the
  floor at each side of the opening.

## The setting, as fixed by the hero

Modern warehouse interior: light-grey insulated wall panels, polished grey
concrete floor with yellow line markings, pallet racking and a plain
forklift visible through the vision band, bright white LED lighting, steel
roof structure. Clean commercial product photography.

## Rules every view must obey

- Same blue curtain, single vision band, stainless hood, guides, control box
  and bollards as the hero. A view that adds a second vision strip, exposes
  a bare roller drum, changes the curtain colour or removes the hood is
  rejected.
- No text, signage, logos, brand names or badges anywhere — including the
  hood, the control box, the curtain and any vehicle.
- Vehicles are plain and unmarked. No people.
- No speeds, dimensions, ratings or any specification implied visually.
- Interim visualisations, replaced by installation photography under the same
  filenames when it exists.
