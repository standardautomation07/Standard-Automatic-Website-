- **Bottom bar** — slim aluminium bottom rail with a black soft seal.
- **Controls** — light-grey wall-mounted control box to the right of the

- **Protection** — a yellow-and-black banded steel bollard at each side.

## The self-repairing mechanism, as the product copy describes it

The curtain is held in *flexible self-repairing guides rather than a fixed
retention profile*; on impact it leaves the guide instead of tearing, and it
*re-enters the guide system automatically* at the top of travel. Views show
this and nothing beyond it:

- a welded retaining bead along each curtain edge, running inside the black
  flexible channel of the guide;
- the bead pulled out of the channel where the curtain has been pushed,
  the curtain itself undamaged;
- the flared re-entry mouth at the head of the guide that re-threads the
  bead as the curtain runs up and down again.

No cutaways of the drive, no springs, cables or internals the business has
not specified.
