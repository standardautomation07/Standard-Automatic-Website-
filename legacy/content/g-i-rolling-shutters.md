### GALVANIZE ROLLING SHUTTER :

PRODUCT CODE : GLRS78INS 
 Our GL78INS model of insulated roller shutter most impressive product yet – moving almost silently and doing away with the old rattling noises that roller shutters traditionally make. 
It is made from double skinned polyester powder coated 78mm slat with a polyurethane foam insulating foam core ensuring the best in insulation and keeping your energy costs down and your work shop warm; whilst also doing a pretty good job at keeping the sound out.
It comes in 8 colours as standard (Blue, Red, Mid-grey, Silver, White, Brown, Fir green, Light grey) but non-standard colours are available over a galvanized finish on request.

### SHUTTER CURTAIN

Double skinned polyester powder coated 78mm slat with polyurethane foam insulating core. 
 Colour: Galvanised finish as standard. Plastisol range or powder coating available. 

 Profile Height: 78mm 
 Weight per m2: 12kg 
 Maximum Width: 5,800mm 
 Maximum Surface: 35m²

### VERTICAL SIDE GUIDES / BOTTOM SLAT

Aluminium powder coated bottom slat with EPDM weather seal. Guide channel depth is 75mm as standard. 
Colour: Galvanised finish as standard. Plastisol range or powder coating available. 
 Note: The guide channels are bolted to fixing angles or box section to suit the application.

### ENCLOSURE / HOUSING

Manufactured from 0.9mm galvanised steel sheet. End caps are fabricated from 3mm - 12mm steel. 
 Colour: Galvanised finish as standard. Plastisol range or powder coating available. 
 OPERATION 
 Electric: Single phase tubular motor or 3-phase outboard motor with push button control. Numerous options such as group or remote control etc available as required. Manual override facility as standard. 
 CE compliant to all applicable directives and regulations.

### ALUMINUM INSUALTED ROLLING SHUTTER:

PRODUCT CODE : ALURS77INS 
 Our Alumnium rolling shutters come insulated which make them the perfect product for customers who want to save on energy bills as they keep the heat in, cold out.They are also used as “high quality” curtains and block out nearly 100% of all light – ideal for those of you living in big cities
Comes in white or brown as standard, but can be powder coated a number of colours for a surcharge.

### Housing

Manufactured from roll formed aluminium with a stove-enamelled finish in 2 sections with a 45° chamfered lid. Circular housing option.

### Guide Channels / Bottom Slat

Extruded aluminium with weather seal. Circular guide option. 53mm, 60mm or 68mm depths available as required.
