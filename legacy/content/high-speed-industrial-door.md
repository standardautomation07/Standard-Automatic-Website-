### High Speed Industrial Door

Metal high speed turbine fast door is new type of metal industry door, which have many functions such as:heat preservation and energy saving, seal, high efficiency, wind resistance, environmental protection . etc . And the open speed is up to 2.5 meters per second, it is suitable for frequently high-speed logistics channel. Door plank is made of 2.0 mm thick double layer heat insulation broken bridge aluminum composite forming, the middle material is Owens Corning brand fire insulation corning glass.
