### Brief Description

Galvanize steel shutters are used to help prevent unauthorized access and vandalism to premises by adding an extra barrier between “outside” and whatever entrances they may be protecting. 
When open, the shutters provide an almost unobstructed aperture as the shutter and mechanism store above the doorway and out of the way. They can be supplied up to 10000 mm wide.
