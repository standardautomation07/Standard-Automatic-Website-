Insulated Rolling Shutters

TYPES OF INSULATED ROLLING SHUTTER :

  • GALVANIZE INSULATED ROLLING SHUTTER
  • ALUMNIUM INSULATED ROLLING SHUTTER

GALVANIZE ROLLING SHUTTER :


PRODUCT CODE : GLRS78INS

Our GL78INS model of insulated roller shutter most impressive product yet – moving almost silently and doing away with the old rattling noises that roller shutters traditionally make. It is made from double skinned polyester powder coated 78mm slat with a polyurethane foam insulating foam core ensuring the best in insulation and keeping your energy costs down and your work shop warm; whilst also doing a pretty good job at keeping the sound out. It comes in 8 colours as standard (Blue, Red, Mid-grey, Silver, White, Brown, Fir green, Light grey) but non-standard colours are available over a galvanized finish on request.

Ideal uses / applications

  • Warehouses
  • Factories
  • Loading bays
  • Other areas where a high level of security is required as well as a high degree of insulation

Key Facts

CLICK to reveal more information
  • Comes in 8 popular colours free of charge
  • Very little rattling in operation
  • Each lath has a U-Value of 1.1
  • Specification/Technical Data

SHUTTER CURTAIN

Double skinned polyester powder coated 78mm slat with polyurethane foam insulating core.

Colour: Galvanised finish as standard. Plastisol range or powder coating available.

  • Profile Height: 78mm
  • Weight per m2: 12kg
  • Maximum Width: 5,800mm
  • Maximum Surface: 35m²

VERTICAL SIDE GUIDES / BOTTOM SLAT

Aluminium powder coated bottom slat with EPDM weather seal. Guide channel depth is 75mm as standard.
Colour: Galvanised finish as standard. Plastisol range or powder coating available.
Note: The guide channels are bolted to fixing angles or box section to suit the application.

ENCLOSURE / HOUSING

Manufactured from 0.9mm galvanised steel sheet. End caps are fabricated from 3mm - 12mm steel.
Colour: Galvanised finish as standard. Plastisol range or powder coating available.

OPERATION

Electric: Single phase tubular motor or 3-phase outboard motor with push button control. Numerous options such as group or remote control etc available as required. Manual override facility as standard. CE compliant to all applicable directives and regulations.

ALUMINUM INSUALTED ROLLING SHUTTER:


PRODUCT CODE : ALURS77INS

Our Alumnium rolling shutters come insulated which make them the perfect product for customers who want to save on energy bills as they keep the heat in, cold out.They are also used as “high quality” curtains and block out nearly 100% of all light – ideal for those of you living in big cities Comes in white or brown as standard, but can be powder coated a number of colours for a surcharge.

Application/Ideal Use :

Our ALURS55/ALURS77 shutters are often used in:
  • House windows
  • Office windows
  • Reception counters
  • Display areas
  • Shop fronts
  • And homes in cupboards due to their natural aesthetically pleasing design.

Key Facts

  • Offers low to Medium Security
  • The ALURS55/77 traditionally offers a more cost effective and aesthetically pleasing solution to that of an insulated steel shutter.
  • Lighter than a steel shutter means less energy is needed to open them (3.5kg/m2 approx).
  • Comes in white or brown as standard, but can be powder coated a number of colours for a surcharge.
  • Maximum width 5000 mm
  • Maximum surface 15 m2

Applications

Low to Medium security domestic sites with a maximum size of 15M2

Curtain Slat

  • Alumininium double skinned polyester powder coated slat with polyurethane foam insulating core. Vented or non-vented options
  • Standard colours: White, Brown, Beige, Silver. Other colours available on request
  • Blind Surface: 55 mm
  • Weight per m2 5.5 kg/
  • Maximum Width 5000mm
  • Maximum Surface 15m2

CURATAIN PROFILE :

Housing

  • Manufactured from roll formed aluminium with a stove-enamelled finish in 2 sections with a 45° chamfered lid. Circular housing option.
  • End caps are pressure moulded aluminium alloy.
  • Standard colours : White, or Brown. Other colours available on request.

Guide Channels / Bottom Slat

Extruded aluminium with weather seal. Circular guide option. 53mm, 60mm or 68mm depths available as required.

Standard colours White or Brown. Other colours available on request.

Operation

Electric: Single phase tubular motor & wall switch as standard. Numerous options such as group or remote control etc. Manual override facility as required.
