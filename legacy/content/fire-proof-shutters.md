### Aluminium Rolling Shutters

Our Alumnium rolling shutters come insulated which make them the perfect product for customers who want to save on energy bills as they keep the heat in, cold out.They are also used as “high quality” curtains and block out nearly 100% of all light – ideal for those of you living in big cities
Comes in white or brown as standard, but can be powder coated a number of colours for a surcharge.

### Application/Ideal Use :

Our ALURS55/ALURS77 shutters are often used in: 

 House windows 
 Office windows 
 Reception counters 
 Display areas 
 Shop fronts 
 And homes in cupboards due to their natural aesthetically pleasing design.

### Key facts :

Offers low to Medium Security 
 The ALURS55/77 traditionally offers a more cost effective and aesthetically pleasing solution to that of an insulated steel shutter. 
 Lighter than a steel shutter means less energy is needed to open them (3.5kg/m2 approx). 
 Comes in white or brown as standard, but can be powder coated a number of colours for a surcharge. 
 Maximum width 5000 mm 
 Maximum surface 15 m2

### Applications

Low to Medium security domestic sites with a maximum size of 15M2

### Curtain Slat

Alumininium double skinned polyester powder coated slat with polyurethane foam insulating core. Vented or non-vented options 
 Standard colours: White, Brown, Beige, Silver. Other colours available on request 
 Blind Surface: 55 mm 
 Weight per m2 5.5 kg/ 
 Maximum Width 5000mm 
 Maximum Surface 15m2

### Housing

Manufactured from roll formed aluminium with a stove-enamelled finish in 2 sections with a 45° chamfered lid. Circular housing option. 
 End caps are pressure moulded aluminium alloy. 
 Standard colours : White, or Brown. Other colours available on request.

### Guide Channels / Bottom Slat

Extruded aluminium with weather seal. Circular guide option. 53mm, 60mm or 68mm depths available as required.

### Finish

Standard colours White or Brown. Other colours available on request.

### Operation

Electric: Single phase tubular motor & wall switch as standard. Numerous options such as group or remote control etc. Manual override facility as required.

### Hoods/Fascia’s

Powder coated aluminum hoods come as standard with our products.
