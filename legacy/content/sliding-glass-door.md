### Sliding Glass Doors

Automatic Sliding Door are installed in reception area, mall entrance or any location of business hub to provide easy pass through for people where there is continues movement. It also maintains temperature inside the premises and save energy.
It can be installed in any area of residential, commercial and industrial sector. Wide ranges of models are available depending on the weight & dimension of doors. Electronic logic controller complete of all the devices for the full control of the operator power supply, the logic control for the photocell, the predisposition for the battery emergency device & can be set to adjust operating speed, the opening & closing speed, partial opening etc.
The motor controlled by the electronic units transmits the movement to the leaves through a drive belt. Less maintenance required for automation system.

Manual operation in case of power failure
