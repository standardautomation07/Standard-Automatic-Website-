### High Speed Fold Up Doors

High Speed Fold Up Doors are basically applied for quick passage, minimum spaces, wind resistance, protection from dust, maintenance free. On a pre-painted galvanised steel structure, fold up door its a tear-proof double layer PVC closing curtain, class 2 fireproof, with possible transparent windows allowing for personnel to see beyond the door. The elegant design and the range of personalisation available make it easily adaptable to any context and requirement.
